A SECOND OPINION

Fifteen estimators, one placebo test, same answer

Each method runs twice: on the campaign weeks, and on the 8 weeks before, where the true effect is zero.

Nine pass the placebo. Their answer: −$0.1 to +$1.3 a week.

Propensity-score weighting misses the placebo by $8.

ANCOVA, propensity scores four ways, S/T/X-learners, double ML, doubly robust learner, causal forest. Raw gap: +$26.
