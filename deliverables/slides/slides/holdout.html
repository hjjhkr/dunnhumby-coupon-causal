The experiment that would settle it

STEP 1

Take the campaign’s normal target list

STEP 2

Sort it into four tiers by shopping frequency

Blocking

STEP 3

Within each tier, hold out a random 10%

Randomization

STEP 4

Compare spending, adjusting for spending before

ANCOVA

Random assignment leaves no backdoor path to block. Blocking and ANCOVA cut the sample for a 1% lift from 135,000 to 58,000 households per group.

A 2% lift with a 10% holdout needs about 81,000 households in total. 5% significance, 80% power.
