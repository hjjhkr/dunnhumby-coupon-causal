APPENDIX

Method and limitations

SAMPLE

The first 16 weeks are a data ramp-up. Outcomes use weeks 17–101; the 2,269 households present by week 16 are kept.

TREATED AND CONTROLS

Five cohorts receiving a large campaign for the first time: 1,033 households. Controls have no campaign by week 12 after the start.

WEIGHTING

Entropy balancing matches controls to the treated on spending, trips and visit frequency in four pre-campaign periods (12 measures).

ESTIMATOR

Doubly robust: weighting, then regression adjustment. Regression alone gives +1.8%; weighting alone +1.3%.

CONFIDENCE INTERVALS

Household-level bootstrap, 1,000 replications, redoing the weighting and regression each time.

LIMITATIONS

Not a randomized experiment. It assumes households with the same pre-campaign records would have followed the same path without coupons. The check window supports this but cannot prove it.

The full analysis is in coupon_causal_analysis.ipynb and runs end to end from the raw data.
