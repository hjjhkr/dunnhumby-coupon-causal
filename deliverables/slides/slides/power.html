A household spends $46 a week on average, but the change from before to after has a standard deviation of $31. With that much noise, small effects need very large samples.

This study has 1,033 treated households and can reliably detect effects of about 14% or more.

Yet redemptions can have generated at most 0.4% of spending directly.

Randomized test, 5% significance, 80% power. 0.4% assumes every redeemed item was purely incremental.
