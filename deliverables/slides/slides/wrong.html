Extra weekly spending of later redeemers vs controls

Many marketing reports compare coupon users with non-users. Done that way, households that redeemed spend about $8 a week more during the campaign.

But before the campaign started, they were already spending $7.5 more.

Redeeming is the customer’s own choice after the coupon arrives. Splitting on it treats an outcome as a cause, so we compare by who received coupons.
