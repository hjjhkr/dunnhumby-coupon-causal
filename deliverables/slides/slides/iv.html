Like the Google ad test: the mailing is the assignment, redeeming is the take-up. Nobody redeems without a mailing, and 11.5% of recipients redeem.

Effect per redeeming household: +$5 a week, with an interval from −$30 to +$45.

Dividing by a small take-up rate multiplies the uncertainty by nine. And the mailing is not a valid instrument: it was targeted, and it may work as a reminder without any coupon being used.
