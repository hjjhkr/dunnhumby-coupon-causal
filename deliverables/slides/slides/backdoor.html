READING THE GRAPH

Adjust for shopping history, never for redemption

FORK

T ← H → Y

Past shopping causes both the coupon and the spending

Confounding. Block it by adjusting for H.

CHAIN

T → R → Y

Redemption carries part of any effect

A mediator. Leave it alone.

COLLIDER

T → R ← V

Redeemers are households that shopped more that week

Conditioning on R opens a false path.

Backdoor criterion: pre-campaign history blocks every backdoor path, and the campaign cannot have changed it.

T: coupon campaign. H: pre-campaign shopping history. R: redemption. V: shopping in the campaign weeks. Y: spending.
