RECOMMENDATIONS

Four recommendations for the retailer

01

Hold out a random control group

Leave 5–10% of targeted households without coupons, at random, and measure the lift directly.

02

Rethink who gets coupons

Send some to customers whose shopping is middling or falling: a coupon can still change them.

03

Rethink which coupons to send

Only a coupon for a category not yet bought can create a new purchase: 6% of redemptions today.

04

Judge by incremental sales

A high redemption rate says coupons were used, not that they brought in new spending.
